# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Using SQL

A SQL-based data analysis project exploring pizza sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product popularity, and revenue trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses SQL queries to analyze pizza sales data and answer business questions at three levels: Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced.

The analysis focuses on sales metrics, popular pizza types, order patterns, category-wise performance, and revenue trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Calculate total orders and revenue generated from pizza sales.
- Identify the highest-priced pizza and most popular pizza sizes.
- Analyze pizza sales by product type and category.
- Examine order distribution by hour and date.
- Evaluate revenue contribution and cumulative revenue growth.
- Rank top-performing pizza types based on quantity and revenue.

## 🛠️ Tools and Skills

- **Tool:** SQL
- **SQL Concepts:** SELECT, JOIN, WHERE, GROUP BY, ORDER BY, aggregate functions, subqueries, date/time analysis, percentage calculations, cumulative sums, and ranking.
- **Key Skills:** Data analysis, sales performance analysis, revenue analysis, and business insights.

## 📊 Analysis and Business Questions

### 1. Basic Analysis

1. Retrieve the total number of orders placed.
2. Calculate the total revenue generated from pizza sales.
3. Identify the highest-priced pizza.
4. Identify the most common pizza size ordered.
5. List the top 5 most ordered pizza types along with their quantities.

### 2. Intermediate Analysis

1. Join the necessary tables to find the total quantity of each pizza category ordered.
2. Determine the distribution of orders by hour of the day.
3. Join relevant tables to find the category-wise distribution of pizzas.
4. Group orders by date and calculate the average number of pizzas ordered per day.
5. Determine the top 3 most ordered pizza types based on revenue.

### 3. Advanced Analysis

1. Calculate the percentage contribution of each pizza type to total revenue.
2. Analyze cumulative revenue generated over time.
3. Determine the top 3 most ordered pizza types based on revenue for each pizza category.

## 💡 Key Learning Outcomes

- Practiced SQL queries for extracting and analyzing sales data.
- Used joins and aggregate functions to combine data and calculate business metrics.
- Analyzed ordering patterns across different times and pizza categories.
- Applied revenue calculations and ranking techniques to evaluate product performance.
- Strengthened SQL-based problem-solving and data analysis skills.
